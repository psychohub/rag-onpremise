HOW TO READ THIS
==========================================================================

  A significant AUC below 0.50 means the score is reliably
  INVERTED: adverse pairs rank above genuine paraphrases.

  A non-significant AUC near 0.50 means the score carries no
  usable information for this contrast. That is the claim to
  make -- not that the model 'performs worse', which the
  data does not support.

  An AUC near 1.00 with a negative or near-zero margin means
  the ordering is right but the cut point is not usable. A
  cache needs the cut point, not the ordering.

  The jackknife is not a confidence interval. It answers one
  question: does this margin depend on a single observation.
